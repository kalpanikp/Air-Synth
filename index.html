AIR SYNTH

Play beats and synths live with your hands. Left hand runs the drums, right hand plays the instrument. Everything stays locked to the key and the beat.

LEFT HAND · BEATS

1–5 fingers = drum layers
Fist = drop · open = slam back
Pinch + up/down = drum filter

RIGHT HAND · SYNTH

Pinch thumb + index = play
Left ↔ right = note · up ↕ down = filter
Spread other fingers = reverb/delay

Works on a laptop (Chrome) or phone. On a phone, put it on a stand about 60–80 cm away, landscape, so both hands are free. On iPhone, turn the silent switch off. Use good light on your hands. The camera feed stays on your device and is never uploaded.

HOW TO PLAY

LEFT HAND — BEATS

  • Hold up 1–5 fingers to set drum layers: kick → clap → hats → percussion → full groove. Changes land on the next beat.
  • Fist = DROP. The beat cuts and the mix goes muffled. Open your hand and the beat slams back on the next bar with an impact.
  • Pinch + move up/down sweeps a filter over the drums. It stays where you let go.

RIGHT HAND — INSTRUMENT

  • Pinch thumb + index to play. Left ↔ right picks the note or chord (always in key). Up ↕ down sets the filter.
  • While pinching, spread your other fingers to add reverb and delay. Curl them to keep it dry.
  • Lead mono lead with glide · Bass acid bass · Arp rolling arpeggio, spread fingers = more octaves · Chords diatonic chords · Theremin continuous pitch · FX DJ filter: down = low-pass, up = high-pass, right = delay throw.

BOTH HANDS

  • Pinch with both hands and hold to start a RISER. Pull your hands apart to build it. Let go and the impact lands on the next bar.

SCENES · LOOPER · REC

  • Intro filtered, 2 layers max · Groove full · Build 4-bar snare roll and riser, then auto-drop · Drop everything in · Break drums out.
  • Loop: press once and it records 4 bars of your playing from the next bar, then loops it. Press again to overdub another pass. ✕ clears it.
  • Rec records the master output and downloads a .webm audio file when you stop.
  • Calibrate: move both hands across your full comfortable reach for 6 seconds.
